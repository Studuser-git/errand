Summary & problem

Today

Generic to-do lists and paper

They don't show which tasks happen at the same place or how long a trip takes. People go back to the same shop twice or misjudge the time they need.

→
Errand Run

A list shaped like a trip

Errands are grouped by stop, each stop has a time estimate, and the header totals the whole run.

3

Goals and non-goals

Goals
  • Add an errand in under 5 seconds
  • Group errands by stop automatically
  • Show total time left at a glance
  • Make overdue and due-today errands easy to spot
  • Work offline, no sign-up, data stays on the device
Not in v1
  • Accounts, sync or sharing
  • Maps, addresses, routing or travel time
  • Reminders or push notifications
  • Recurring errands
4

Who it's for

Busy individual

Runs several errands on a day off and wants to get them all done in one trip.

Household planner

Keeps a running list of things to pick up or drop off during the week.

8

Non-functional requirements

Platform

One self-contained HTML page for modern desktop and mobile browsers.

Responsive

Works from 400 px. Form stacks below 480 px. No sideways scroll.

Theming

Light and dark, following the system or an explicit choice.

Accessibility

Labelled controls, visible focus, "Mark X done" checkbox names, aria-live summary, reduced motion.

Performance

No frameworks. Google Fonts is the only external resource, with system fallbacks.

Privacy

No data leaves the device.
